## Deliberate aligned-layout deviation

PLAN §4.2 requested a raw-versus-aligned Q2_K A/B. M3 stops at raw layout deliberately rather than silently narrowing that gate. Raw reaches 300.23 GB/s, 97.8% of pinned llama.cpp's measured 307 GB/s Q2_K MMVQ result. Even an optimistic 25% reduction of the captured 14.898 µs Q2 pipeline would save only 3.72 µs per layer and roughly 0.16 ms across 43 layers, about 1.2% of the 13.3 ms/token screening anchor. That cannot change the M2 keep/stop decision, while an aligned derived artifact would add loader and checksum surface. The A/B is therefore declined on causal-budget and complexity grounds; raw GGUF Q2_K remains the production contract.
